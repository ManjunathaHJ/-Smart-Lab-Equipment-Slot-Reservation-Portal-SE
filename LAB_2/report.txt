============================================================
1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
============================================================

The Smart Lab Equipment Slot Reservation Portal is designed to
help students reserve laboratory equipment for specific time
slots while allowing technicians to manage equipment availability,
calibration status, and returns.

The system aims to reduce double bookings, ensure that only
properly calibrated equipment is reserved, and maintain accurate
records of equipment usage.

============================================================
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
============================================================

Laboratory equipment may have limited availability and must be
properly managed to avoid conflicts and misuse.

The proposed portal provides a centralized system through which
students can view equipment availability, search and filter
equipment, reserve available slots, and receive reservation
confirmation.

Technicians can update equipment availability and calibration
information, while equipment returns and late returns can also
be tracked.

============================================================
3. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

FR001:
Authenticated students shall be able to view real-time equipment
availability and reserve equipment for a maximum two-hour slot,
up to seven days in advance.

FR002:
The system shall verify the calibration status of equipment before
confirming a reservation.

FR003:
The system shall prevent overlapping reservations for the same
equipment and time slot.

FR004:
The system shall record equipment returns and identify late
returns.

FR005:
Technicians shall be able to update equipment availability and
calibration status.

============================================================
4. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

NFR001:
Concurrent reservation requests should be handled within 200 ms
using appropriate database-level locking/isolation.

NFR002:
Confirmed reservation records and reservation tokens should
persist without data loss.

============================================================
5. MAIN USE CASE
============================================================

Use Case: View Equipment Availability

Actor:
Student

Precondition:
The student is authenticated and logged into the system.

Main Flow:
1. Student logs into the portal.
2. Student selects "View Equipment Availability".
3. System displays available laboratory equipment and slots.
4. Student searches or filters the equipment list.
5. System displays the matching equipment and available slots.

The availability information can then be used by the student to
select an appropriate equipment slot for reservation.
